## What's missing for Ops + LSS positioning (the real gap)

This is the section that matters most. The current site shows you build tools. It doesn't yet show you **improve processes** — which is what an Ops Analyst or LSS Green Belt hire is evaluated on.

### Gap 1 — No metrics anywhere
Every project description is qualitative ("cut handle time", "spot drop-off points"). For ops/process roles, recruiters scan for numbers.

**Examples of what to add:**
- KPI Calculator: "Replaced a 40-minute weekly manual roll-up with a 3-second calculation."
- Email Library: "Cut average handle time per ticket by ~X minutes; reduced inconsistent responses to zero."
- Onboarding Mapper: "Surfaced 3 drop-off points in the onboarding flow; informed a redesign that improved [metric] by X%."

Even *approximate* numbers ("~30%", "weekly → daily") beat no numbers.

### Gap 2 — LSS Green Belt is invisible
You're working on a Green Belt project (Screenwise younger panel compliance). The portfolio doesn't mention LSS, DMAIC, Six Sigma, or your certification path anywhere. For a process improvement role, this is the headline credential.

**Fix:** Add an LSS badge to the hero meta strip ("Lean Six Sigma Green Belt — in progress" or similar) and frame at least one project with DMAIC language ("Defined → Measured → Analysed → Improved → Controlled").

### Gap 3 — No case study depth
Each project is a card → a link to a live tool. There's no middle layer that explains the **problem, approach, and outcome** in 200 words. Recruiters who don't want to play with a live tool have nowhere to read about your thinking.

**Fix:** Add a `/case-studies/` section or expand each card with a "Read the story" toggle. Even three short writeups (problem / approach / result, 150 words each) would transform the portfolio's credibility.

### Gap 4 — Skill cards are generic
Current cards: Data & Analysis · Tools & Automation · Operations · How I Work.

The "Operations" card lists SOPs, process mapping, cross-team collaboration — all good. But it's missing the **methodology vocabulary** recruiters search for: *DMAIC, Lean, Six Sigma, root cause analysis, SIPOC, fishbone, control plan, RACI, continuous improvement, stakeholder management.*

### Gap 5 — No "Selected Outcomes" or stats strip
Most ops portfolios open with a numbers strip: *"3 internal tools shipped · ~X hours/month saved · 4 cross-functional projects led."* Yours doesn't have one. Easy win.

### Gap 6 — Comms Mapping and Line Test Support not yet shown
Per your existing `PORTFOLIO_PLAN.md`, these are intended additions. Comms Mapping in particular is your strongest *systematisation* story — and it's not on the site.

### Gap 7 — No "About me as a candidate" framing
The About section reads as a job description ("My work sits across operations, onboarding, and workflow design"). It doesn't say *what you're looking for next.* The Contact section mentions "full-time role" — that signal could come earlier.

## What recruiters will see in 5 seconds (the squint test)

If a recruiter spends 5 seconds before deciding to scroll:
- ✅ Name, role, location — yes, all visible
- ✅ Visual polish — yes, looks professional
- ⚠️ What kind of role you want — implied ("Panel Service Executive"), not stated
- ❌ Credentials / certifications — none visible
- ❌ Quantified impact — none visible
- ⚠️ Proof of work — yes, but behind clicks

**The hero is doing tone work but not credential work.** Fixable.

## Accessibility quick-check

- ✅ Semantic HTML (`<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`).
- ✅ Alt text on profile image.
- ⚠️ Color contrast: teal `#2a7d8c` on white is 4.5:1 (borderline AA for normal text). Buttons fine.
- ❌ No `aria-label` on nav. No skip link.
- ❌ Icons inside buttons missing `aria-hidden="true"`.
- ⚠️ `aos="fade-up"` can be problematic for users with reduced-motion preference. No `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.

Not blockers for v1 publish — flag for v1.1.
